The boundary

Sweeper Math keeps leaf-volume, lap-time and season-pile arithmetic only. It gives no verdicts about what any sweeper can pick up - lawn condition, wet leaves and machine limits are the manual's call.

The hopper holds twenty-five cubic feet. The maples drop what they drop. Sweeper Math does the honest arithmetic: how many times the hopper fills, how long the laps actually run, and how tall the pile stands when the season ends.

Three panels. The hopper truth turns leaf fall into fills. The lap truth divides your lawn by sweep width and pace. The season truth multiplies weekends into the pile you'll be explaining to the neighbors.
